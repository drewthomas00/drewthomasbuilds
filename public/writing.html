Things that broke in a way I had to go and understand, written down while the detail was still fresh. Mostly Postgres, background jobs, and the parts of compliance that turn out to be engineering problems.

  • 13 August 2026 · Postgres NOW() is not now Postgres NOW() is transaction_timestamp(), frozen when the transaction opened. In a distributed lease that makes an expired lock read as still held — silently, in one direction, and only once somebody passes a transaction handle.
  • 13 August 2026 · Debugging Which of these 38 processes is eating 20 GB? The reload that saves your machine destroys the evidence. Sampling the editor's process tree per tier found the leak in four hours — one renderer, a gigabyte an hour, a monotonic floor under a GC sawtooth.
  • 13 August 2026 · Webhooks Four ways a valid Twilio signature looks invalid Twilio signs the exact public URL it POSTs to. Behind a proxy, four independent things change that URL — and all four report the same single bit of information back.
  • 13 August 2026 · Data 2024-01-15 is not a ZIP code It geocodes anyway, to a real point in Washington DC. A wrong coordinate is worse than none, because downstream nothing can tell it from a right one.
  • 13 August 2026 · Compliance Quiet hours are not your hours The TCPA window is the recipient's local time, the window is not 8-to-9 everywhere, and area codes follow people across timezones for a decade.
